Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/3 cup vinegar
  • 1 head garlic
  • 3 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp peppercorns

How to cook

  1. Marinate the chicken in soy sauce and garlic for 30 minutes.
  2. Brown the chicken in a hot pan, then add the marinade, vinegar, bay leaves and peppercorns.
  3. Simmer for 30 minutes, until tender and the sauce thickens.
  4. Serve hot over steamed rice.
